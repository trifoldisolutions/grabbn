What's a bucket?

A named collection of links — organised, shareable, and optionally monetised.

Organised

Group links by topic, project, or niche. Every bucket has a clear name so you — and your audience — always know what's inside.

Shareable

One link. Anyone can view your public bucket — or grab it to add to their own collection. No account needed to browse.

Monetised

Set a credit price on any bucket. Buyers pay to unlock the full list — and you earn 80% of every sale, automatically.

How it works

From zero to a shareable collection in three steps.

1

Create a bucket

Give it a name that describes your curation — tools, articles, resources, anything.

2

Grab links from any URL

Paste a URL and Grabbn pulls every link from the page. Select the ones that matter.

3

Share or sell

Make it public, set a price, and share the link. Your audience grabs it with one click.
